🗣️ ESSENTIAL JAPANESE PHRASES 🗣️

👋 Greetings & Basics

Konnichiwa (こんにちは)
kohn-nee-chee-wah
Hello/Good afternoon (Late morning - evening)
Arigatou gozaimasu (ありがとうございます)
ah-ree-gah-toh goh-zah-ee-mahs
Thank you (polite) - Add a small bow
Sumimasen (すみません)
soo-mee-mah-sen
Excuse me / I'm sorry - Get attention/apologize
Onegaishimasu (お願いします)
oh-neh-gah-ee-shee-mahs
Please - Add after any request
Hai / Iie (はい / いいえ)
hai / ee-eh
Yes / No (Hai also means "I understand")
Chotto matte kudasai (ちょっと待ってください)
choht-toh maht-teh koo-dah-sai
Please wait a moment

👂 Understanding

Wakarimasen (わかりません)
wah-kah-ree-mah-sen
I don't understand (Polite/apologetic tone)
Wakarimashita (わかりました)
wah-kah-ree-mahsh-tah
I understand / Got it
Eigo ga wakarimasu ka? (英語がわかりますか)
eh-goh gah wah-kah-ree-mahs kah
Do you understand English?
Nihongo ga sukoshi dake (日本語が少しだけ)
nee-hohn-goh gah skoh-shee dah-keh
I only speak a little Japanese
Mou ichido onegaishimasu (もう一度お願いします)
moh ee-chee-doh oh-neh-gah-ee-shee-mahs
One more time, please

🍜 At Restaurants

Itadakimasu (いただきます)
ee-tah-dah-kee-mahs
"I humbly receive" (Before eating)
Gochisousama deshita (ごちそうさまでした)
goh-chee-soh-sah-mah desh-tah
Said after eating (to staff)
Oishii (美味しい)
oy-shee
Delicious!
Hitori desu / Futari desu (一人です / 二人です)
hee-toh-ree des / foo-tah-ree des
One person / Two people
Osusume wa arimasu ka? (おすすめはありますか)
oh-soo-soo-meh wah ah-ree-mahs kah
Do you have any recommendations?
Kore (o) kudasai (これ(を)ください)
koh-reh (oh) koo-dah-sai
I'll have this one (Point at menu)
Okaikei onegaishimasu (お会計お願いします)
oh-kai-keh oh-neh-gah-ee-shee-mahs
Check, please

💳 Payment & Shopping

Kurejitto kaado tsukaemasu ka?
koo-reh-jeet-toh kah-doh tsoo-kah-eh-mahs kah
Can I use a credit card?
IC kaado tsukaemasu ka?
ai-shee kah-doh tsoo-kah-eh-mahs kah
Can I use an IC card (Suica/Pasmo)?
Ikura desu ka? (いくらですか)
ee-koo-rah des kah
How much is it?
Fukuro onegaishimasu (袋お願いします)
foo-koo-roh oh-neh-gah-ee-shee-mahs
A bag, please
Kore o misete kudasai (これを見せてください)
koh-reh oh mee-seh-teh koo-dah-sai
Please show me this

🗺️ Navigation & Help

...wa doko desu ka? (…はどこですか)
wah doh-koh des kah
Where is...? (Insert place name before "wa")
Toire wa doko desu ka? (トイレはどこですか)
toy-reh wah doh-koh des kah
Where is the restroom?
Iie, daijoubu desu (いいえ、大丈夫です)
ee-eh, dai-joh-boo des
No, I'm okay / No thank you (Decline offers)
Shashin o totte moratte mo ii desu ka?
shah-sheen oh toht-teh moh-raht-teh moh ee des kah
May I ask you to take a photo?
Yoyaku ga arimasu (予約があります)
yoh-yah-koo gah ah-ree-mahs
I have a reservation

💡 Pro Tips

  • Speak Softly: Volume matters — speak softly on trains and in public.
  • Bow Slightly: A small bow when thanking someone goes a long way.
  • Pronunciation: Many phrases end in "masu/desu" — pronounce as "mahs/des" (the 'u' is nearly silent).
  • Magic Combo: "Sumimasen" before a question and "Arigatou gozaimasu" after help.
  • Effort Counts: Speaking even a few words in Japanese delights locals!

🗺️ HOW TO FIND RAMEN STREET:
1️⃣ At Tokyo Station, follow signs for Yaesu Exit (八重洲口)
2️⃣ Go THROUGH the ticket gates (EXIT the ticketed area)
3️⃣ Look for stairs going DOWN to B1 basement level
4️⃣ Follow signs for "First Avenue Tokyo Station"
5️⃣ You'll see queues - that's Ramen Street!

ℹ️ Near Tokyo Character Street in basement
ℹ️ If lost, ask station staff - they know it well!

Booking notes

Flights: book as one multi-city ticket on delta.com (SEA→LHR / BCN→SEA) — it prices like a round trip and both legs are nonstop Delta. The Barcelona route is seasonal (through Oct 22) and flies Wed/Fri/Sun; Oct 4 is a Sunday, so it lines up — confirm the date shows when booking.

Concert: BIGBANG 2026 World Tour, Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, Sat Sep 26, 7 PM — tickets via Ticketmaster UK / AXS.

Trains: Eurostar (eurostar.com) and the direct Paris–Barcelona TGV (sncf-connect.com) — book as soon as your dates open; the direct Barcelona train sells out. Seats are reserved on both.

Reserve ahead: Sagrada Família timed entry, Louvre/Orsay timed entry, and the Sep 26 pre-concert dinner near the stadium.
